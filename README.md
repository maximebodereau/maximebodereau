# Maxime Bodereau

Product designer in Nantes. I design and prototype creative tools, AI products and the interfaces people use to steer them.

- [Maxime-Moi](https://maxime-moi.maximebodereau.workers.dev/profil/) brings together my work, UX archives and conversational portfolio.
- [Drawesome](https://maxime-bodereau-public.maximebodereau.workers.dev/) is an interactive landing page with a drawing surface.
- [UX Design Skill](https://github.com/maximebodereau/ux-design-skill) brings UX methods into the workflow of AI coding agents.
- [Selected design work on Behance](https://www.behance.net/MAXIMEBODE5063) covers product UX/UI, art direction and editorial design.
